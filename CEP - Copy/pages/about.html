Mission and community purpose

What CyberSafe is

CyberSafe is a community-focused awareness project developed as part of the Community Engagement Programme (CEP). It encourages ordinary Internet users to build practical skills for safer digital life.

Instead of focusing only on abstract security jargon, the project translates daily online habits into understandable actions such as safer password use, privacy planning, scam recognition, and secure transactions.

Project objectives

  • Reduce password misuse and weak account habits.
  • Improve awareness of phishing, scams, and malicious downloads.
  • Encourage safer privacy settings and data minimisation.
  • Build confident, community-led cyber awareness.

Methodology

Research

We base the content on common real-world risks such as password reuse, social engineering, phishing, and unsafe digital transactions.

Action

Participants are encouraged to test their knowledge, review risk points, and apply practical steps in their daily online behaviour.

Community support

Facilitator-friendly materials, resources, and assessment options help local groups improve awareness together.

Evaluation

Pre/post awareness checks and aggregated statistics help identify knowledge gaps and the impact of learning activities.
