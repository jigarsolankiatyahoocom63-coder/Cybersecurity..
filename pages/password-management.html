Password Hygiene

Password Management

It is difficult to remember dozens of unique passwords. That is why password managers are one of the most effective tools for secure account hygiene.

Good password management includes storing credentials securely, updating them when needed, and avoiding reuse. It also means checking whether any of your accounts have been involved in a data breach and rotating those passwords quickly.

Recommended habits

  • Use a reputable password manager to generate and save passwords.
  • Turn on breach alerts and rotate passwords after public incidents.
  • Review access to old and unused accounts.

Important

Even a strong password can cause risk if you reuse it or if a trusted device is compromised. Safe habits include device security, account auditing, and up-to-date recovery methods.

Key takeaways

  • Password managers reduce mistakes caused by weak memory and reuse.
  • Regular review of account security reduces exposure.
  • Do not store login details in notes or plain-text messages.
