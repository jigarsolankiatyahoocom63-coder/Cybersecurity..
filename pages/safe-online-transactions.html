Online Threats

Safe Online Transactions

Online transactions require trust, verification, and a clear understanding of the payment channel. Fraud often succeeds because people click before checking the context or the payment destination.

Many scams are built around urgency such as “confirm payment now”, “your account will be closed”, or “payment is pending”. Real transactions should be verified through official websites or trusted apps.

Safer practices

  • Only use trusted payment portals and official websites.
  • Do not send security codes to anyone claiming to be a support agent.
  • Confirm transaction requests through your own app or official contact channel.

Common traps

  • Fake shipping or refund messages
  • Urgent requests to transfer money or update payment details
  • Requests to approve a login or transaction from an unexpected message

Key takeaways

  • Verify payment requests before approving them.
  • Do not trust urgency as proof of authenticity.
  • Use secure payment methods and avoid unverified links.
