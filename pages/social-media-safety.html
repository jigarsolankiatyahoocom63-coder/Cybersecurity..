Personal Data Protection

Social Media Safety

Social media can be useful for communication and community, but it can also expose personal details to strangers. A single public post can reveal family names, locations, routines, school information, or travel plans.

People often forget that accounts can be cloned, impersonated, or targeted with fake messages. Strong account security and thoughtful privacy choices reduce the risk.

Safer social habits

  • Limit who can view posts and personal details.
  • Review friend request and follow requests carefully.
  • Do not post exact locations while travelling.

Watch for this

Scammers often use publicly available personal details to trick people into thinking they know the victim personally.

Key takeaways

  • Public profiles are part of the attack surface.
  • Less public detail means fewer opportunities for impersonation.
  • Privacy settings should be checked regularly, not just once.
