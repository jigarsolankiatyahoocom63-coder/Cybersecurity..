Password Hygiene

Introduction to Password Hygiene

Your password is often the first barrier between your personal life and attackers. Many people think a password is only about remembering a word, but good password hygiene is about reducing risk across all accounts.

In real life, attackers do not need to guess your password perfectly. They often rely on reused credentials, weak structures, social engineering, or hacked data leaks. A password that is short, common, or repeated elsewhere can become a major weakness.

Why it matters

  • Password reuse allows one breach to spread across accounts.
  • Weak passwords are easy for guessing and brute-force attacks.
  • Attackers often target email, banking, social media, and work platforms.

Common mistake

Using the same password for email, social media, and banking makes it easier for attackers to exploit one compromise across multiple services.

Key takeaways

  • Never reuse the same password across important accounts.
  • Use longer passphrases instead of short, predictable words.
  • Turn on 2FA where possible.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords safely.
