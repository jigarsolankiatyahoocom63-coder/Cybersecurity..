Personal Data Protection

Personal data includes your name, address, phone number, date of birth, identity numbers, and even routine details like your school, workplace, and travel patterns. Attackers may combine these pieces to steal your identity, reset passwords, or impersonate you.

The best approach is to reduce unnecessary sharing. If a service or app does not need a detail, it should not collect it or request it in a public form.

Safer habits

  • Limit public profile information.
  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Do not share ID or account details in public groups.

Watch for this

Public posts about birthdays, school names, and sensitive life events may help attackers make convincing scam messages.

Key takeaways

  • Reduce unnecessary sharing and question every request for personal details.
  • Use privacy settings on social media and messaging platforms.
  • Verify any request for personal information through a trusted channel.
