Online Threats

Common Cyber Scams

Scams often rely on emotional triggers such as fear, excitement, trust, or urgency. Attackers may claim to be banks, travel agencies, recruitment teams, service providers, or even friends whose accounts were compromised.

These scams often begin with a message or a message thread that feels normal until the victim is pressured to act quickly. The best protection is to pause and verify the request using the official information source.

Typical scam patterns

  • Unexpected prize or reward claims
  • Fake refunds or refund reversal notices
  • Delivery or service issues requiring urgent action
  • Requests for payment in unusual methods

Use these habits

  • Pause before responding to urgent requests.
  • Check official contact details independently.
  • Never send OTP codes or passwords to requests you did not verify.

Key takeaways

  • Scams depend on urgency and pressure.
  • Verification is more important than speed.
  • People rarely lose money because of one bad click; they lose it because they did not pause to verify.
