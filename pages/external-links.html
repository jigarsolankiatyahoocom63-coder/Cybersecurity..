Official Indian reporting services, plus the cybersecurity resources referenced in the campaign report.

Urgent financial cyber fraud? Call the national helpline 1930 immediately, then submit a report at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Report online financial fraud, cybercrime against women or children, and other cyber incidents to the Government of India.

Find national cybersecurity alerts and incident-response guidance, and follow CERT-In's official instructions for reporting security incidents.

RBI Complaint Management System

Escalate an unresolved complaint about a bank, payment system, or regulated financial service through the Reserve Bank of India's portal.

Submit an RBI complaint

National Consumer Helpline

Seek help with consumer grievances, including problems with online purchases, e-commerce services, or transactions with a business.

Register a consumer grievance
