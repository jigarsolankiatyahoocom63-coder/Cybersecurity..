Top safety reminder

Pause before you click. Verify before you share. Protect before you trust.

Password habit card

Use a unique password for each account, and store it in a trusted password manager.

Privacy habit card

Limit public information and check your privacy settings on a regular schedule.

Scam warning card

Ask: Is this message expected? Is the request real? Did I verify directly?

Printable summary

  • Use a strong, unique password for every important account.
  • Turn on 2FA wherever available.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, or sensitive details with unexpected messages.
  • Verify payment and security requests using official service channels.
  • Review privacy and permissions on social media, apps, and messaging tools.
